My Dearest Mamma,

I want you to know that I understand that you've been through things that have hurt you deeply. I may never fully understand how much those experiences affected you, but I don't want you to feel that you have to carry all of that alone.

I didn't choose you because of what people said about you. Ki briew te phi lah tip hi em kin kren beit, sometimes kin pynduh mysniem ruh lada ki bishni. I chose you because I got to know you, because I connected with you, because I saw something beautiful in you, and ha kaba kut my HEART chose YOU baby. Whatever people say about your past, or my past too, they don't get to define who you are or what you deserve..

I'm not asking for 24 hours of your time, baby. I know we both have responsibilities and lives of our own. Nga khlem ong ruh ba phin long available man ki second jong ka sngi. I have never loved anyone the way I love you. I want to FEEL that I have a place in your life, just as you have a place in mine.

I'm not a rich man, Mamma, and ioh lehse ngam lah re ban ai everything in this world, but whatever I have, whatever I can do, I want to give it to you with all my heart. I want to make you happy, make you feel appreciated, and show you how SPECIAL YOU ARE TO ME. I thanked GOD every day for this.

I know you're scared that I might CHANGE or that one day I might leave. But I don't want you to judge our future by what someone else did to you in the past, or by what your friends said about me. I'm not asking you to trust me blindly. Let me earn that trust through my actions, consistency and time.

And if you need time to heal, I'll respect that. I don't want to suffocate you lane pressured ia phi ban ieid ia nga. But I also hope you won't shut me out because you're afraid of what might happen.

I want to hold your hands, walk this LIFE with YOU baby. I don't need any messages or calls from anyone. Just having YOU in my life makes me want to LIVE, SMILE and be HAPPY with YOU, MAMMA. The silly moments and the memories we haven't made yet too. Hooid ioh lehse kin sa don ki sngi ba ngim don kyntien ban kren, hynrei nga ngeit ba ngin kmen iwei ia iwei.

You once told me that you hate long distance, Mamma, and I haven't forgotten that. That's one of the reasons why I've tried my best to come and meet you whenever I can, to spend time with you, to be there beside you, and to make sure you don't feel lonely. Sometimes you may not say that you want or need me around, but there are moments when I just feel that you might need me, and I want to be there for you.

I know I cannot always be physically beside you, but I don't want the distance between us to make you feel that you're alone in this relationship. I want us both to keep making an effort, finding little ways to stay connected, and reminding each other that even though we're miles apart, we're still choosing each other.

I’m not a perfect person, ngam lah ban promise easy days ia phi baby, hynrei lada ki wan kita ki hard days ai ngan kdup ia phi bad ngan ong we’re gonna be okay. I'm not expecting a perfect relationship. I'm expecting two IMPERFECT people who are willing to understand each other, make mistakes, communicate and keep choosing each other.

I love you, Mamma. And I genuinely want US to work. But please remember, you don't have to be afraid of being happy. You deserve that happiness too. Let's not allow yesterday's wounds to decide what tomorrow can become. ❤️
